# Alibi 0.6.9

Fixes stepped pinch-to-zoom in photo capture, video preparation previews and live
recording, including the optional standard camera screen.

The old dispatcher waited for the camera's capture-result acknowledgement before
sending the next ratio. On the Huawei Mate 70 Pro this took hundreds of
milliseconds, skipping most of the intermediate eased ratios. Zoom requests now
submit the newest eased ratio at a bounded 32 ms cadence independently of those
acknowledgements. Absolute zoom controls and the final gesture ratio submit
immediately. Late completions cannot replay an old target after a camera switch.

Tests now cover slow/missing acknowledgements, rate limiting, direction changes,
final targets, absolute controls and reset. Device tests observe actual camera
capture results for photo, video preview and recording, checking the number of
intermediate zoom steps, their spacing, their size and final convergence.
Capture-result tracing is present only in Debug builds.
